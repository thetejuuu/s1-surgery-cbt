# S1 R2 2025 — Immediate seniors' paper: extraction record

## What has been imported

The six-page PDF has been extracted into a dedicated source collection in `questions.json`. The extraction is kept separate from the older 125-question bank.

- **54 main-paper MCQ slots**: individually represented, including repeated versions, incomplete items and items with uncertain OCR/layout.
- **27 transcript MCQs**: individually represented.
- **81 MCQ records in total** from this paper.
- **6 theory questions**: retained as separate written prompts in `theory.json`.
- **87 total question entries** across MCQ and theory sections.

## Scoring status

- 38 of the 81 extracted MCQ records have an independently added practice key. The source paper itself does not provide an answer key.
- 43 records are intentionally unkeyed because the source has missing options, incomplete stems, ambiguous wording or extraction uncertainty. They remain available for reading/practice but are excluded from scored accuracy.
- Independently added keys and explanations are labelled in each record; they are not represented as answers printed in the original paper.

## Whole-bank totals

- 125 pre-existing question records.
- 81 newly structured records from the immediate seniors' R2 2025 paper.
- **206 MCQ records in total: 163 keyed and 43 unkeyed.**
- 17 theory prompts in total, including the six from this R2 2025 paper.

## Preservation rules

Question wording and options are retained as transcribed wherever legible. Repeated questions are not silently removed. Missing options are marked as missing rather than invented. The full extracted source text and the original PDF are retained in this package so each item can be checked against its page. PDF text extraction can interleave two-column content; consult the original page where order or wording is uncertain.
